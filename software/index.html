SEAMLESS CLINICAL JOURNEY

The 4-Step Patient Care Workflow

From initial reception check-in to post-consultation billing, every department moves in complete synchrony.

01

Check-In & Token

Reception registers the patient in seconds or scans their existing QR Health Card, dispensing a numbered OPD queue slip with department routing.

02

Doctor Consultation

The doctor reviews the patient's past medical history, records current vitals, logs diagnoses, and prescribes medications directly into the EMR.

03

Procedure & Pharmacy

Clinical procedures or nursing treatments are logged to the patient's dues ledger, while prescribed medicines appear instantly at the in-house dispensary.

04

Consolidated Billing

The patient completes checkout at the front desk. Fees, medicines, and procedures consolidate into one invoice with thermal printout and WhatsApp e-receipt.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers for Healthcare Administrators & Clinic Owners

Technical, operational, and billing questions about deploying Big4Digital HMS in your healthcare practice.

Q. What hardware do we need to run Big4Digital HMS?

Big4Digital HMS runs smoothly on any standard Windows PC, Mac, laptop, tablet, or POS terminal using Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. It supports all standard 80mm and 58mm ESC/POS thermal receipt printers, USB/Bluetooth barcode scanners, and automatic cash drawers.

Q. What happens if our clinic internet connection goes down?

Big4Digital HMS features offline resilience via localized progressive caching. Your front-desk can continue issuing tokens, processing consultations, and billing patients uninterrupted. The moment internet connectivity restores, all transactions automatically sync with the cloud database.

Q. How does the 4-digit PIN discount protection work?

Whenever a receptionist or cashier attempts to apply a concession or percentage discount at checkout, the system locks the action and prompts for a designated 4-digit manager PIN. This prevents staff from giving unauthorized discounts without administrative approval.

Q. Can we track doctor commissions and revenue splits?

Yes. You can assign custom percentage or fixed-fee splits per doctor or per service. When an OPD token or procedure is paid, the system automatically logs the clinic's share and the doctor's payout, generating transparent settlement sheets at the end of each shift or month.

Q. Is our patient medical data private and secure?

Absolutely. Each healthcare facility operates in an isolated tenant environment with encrypted cloud storage, SSL/TLS data in transit, and role-based permissions preventing unauthorized staff from viewing confidential patient records outside their assigned clearance.

Q. How quickly can our clinic onboard and begin using the system?

You can register and launch your live workspace in less than 2 minutes via our portal. Pre-configured medical specialty templates (General OPD, Dental, Dermatology, Pediatrics) allow you to start registering patients and dispensing tokens on day one.
