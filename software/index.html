High-speed checkout terminal designed for retail stores, supermarkets, apparel, and electronics. Handles thousands of SKUs effortlessly with multi-tier pricing.

  • High-speed barcode scanner checkout & label generator
  • Hold Bill (F4) & Parked Sales for busy counters
  • 58mm / 80mm ESC/POS thermal receipt auto-printing
  • Cashier shift register, blind drops & daily Z-Report

Restaurant & Cafe POS

Accelerate food service with interactive table floor plans, Kitchen Order Tickets (KOT) sent to kitchen display screens, and flexible menu modifier sets.

ENTERPRISE ARCHITECTURE

Why Leading Enterprises Rely on Big4Digital

Engineered with modern web standards, offline caching, and bank-grade data security to ensure uninterrupted commercial operations.

Offline-First Resilience

Hardware Compatibility
