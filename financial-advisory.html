Fiscal Governance

Empowering Executive Leadership With Financial Intelligence

Financial clarity is the engine of executive confidence. Our Financial Advisory practice serves as a strategic compass for leadership teams—optimizing working capital cycles, establishing dynamic rolling cash forecasts, and aligning corporate spending with long-term commercial milestones.

Key Capabilities

What We Deliver

✓

Working Capital Optimization

Accelerating cash conversion cycles, optimizing accounts receivable collections, and managing supplier payables.

✓

Dynamic 13-Week Cash Forecasting

Building high-precision rolling cash models to safeguard liquidity and predict capital needs months in advance.

✓

Cost Structure Rationalization

Categorizing fixed versus variable expenditures to identify margin-expanding efficiencies without hindering growth.

✓

Fractional CFO Steering

Senior financial leadership for board meetings, bank covenant negotiations, and strategic resource allocation.

Execution Lifecycle

Our Delivery Methodology

A disciplined, iterative delivery lifecycle engineered for clarity, speed, and accountability.

01

Financial Diagnostic

Auditing current chart of accounts, cash collection velocity, gross margins, and expense structures.

02

Forecast Architecture

Deploying rolling cash flow models, budget variance trackers, and departmental KPI scorecards.

03

Working Capital Tune

Revising customer payment terms, renegotiating vendor lines, and setting minimum liquidity reserves.

04

Executive Reviews

Conducting monthly financial reviews with leadership to evaluate variance against budget and pivot strategy.
