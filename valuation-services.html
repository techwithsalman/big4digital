Asset Appraisal

Defensible Corporate Valuation Built on Empirical Rigor

Whether navigating an equity transaction, corporate restructuring, shareholder dispute, or statutory compliance filing, establishing an accurate, defensible business valuation is paramount. We deploy multi-methodology appraisal models that withstand scrutiny from auditors, tax authorities, and institutional investors.

Key Capabilities

What We Deliver

✓

Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Modeling

Detailed multi-stage projection models calculating weighted average cost of capital (WACC) and terminal value.

✓

Comparable Multiples Analysis

Empirical benchmarking against public peers (EV/EBITDA, P/E) and precedent transaction multiples.

✓

Intangible Asset Valuation

Appraising intellectual property, brand trademarks, customer contracts, and proprietary technologies.

✓

Fairness Opinions & Dispute Support

Providing independent, objective valuation opinions for board reviews, shareholder buyouts, and arbitration.

Execution Lifecycle

Our Delivery Methodology

A disciplined, iterative delivery lifecycle engineered for clarity, speed, and accountability.

01

Information Intake

Reviewing historical audited financial statements, capital expenditure budgets, and growth projections.

02

Industry Benchmarking

Sourcing comparable peer multiples, risk-free discount rates, and sector risk premiums.

03

Multi-Method Modeling

Running DCF, market approach, and asset-based calculations with rigorous sensitivity tables.

04

Valuation Report

Issuing a comprehensive, audit-defensible valuation report detailing assumptions, formulas, and conclusions.
