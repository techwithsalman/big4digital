Service Overview

Effective human resources management balances legal compliance with an inspiring employee experience. We advise growing businesses on constructing institutional HR infrastructure—from employee handbooks and competitive compensation grading to performance appraisal frameworks and labor law compliance.

Key Deliverables & Scope

Our structured engagement model ensures total clarity and accountable deliverables across every project phase:

Comprehensive employee handbooks and statutory workplace policies
Salary benchmarking, grade bands, and total reward structures
Performance appraisal systems, 360-feedback, and OKR frameworks
Labor law compliance audits and workplace dispute protocols
HR tech stack advisory and HRIS implementation support

Commercial Benefits

Partnering with Big4Digital delivers tangible strategic and commercial advantages:

Full legal compliance with local labor laws, minimizing litigation exposure
Competitive compensation structures that attract and retain premier talent
Objective performance management systems tied directly to business results
Standardized onboarding and employee lifecycle workflows

Our 4-Stage Methodology

We execute through a disciplined, battle-tested framework engineered to minimize operational friction and maximize speed to value:

01
HR Infrastructure Audit

Reviewing employment contracts, handbooks, benefit plans, and compliance records.

02
Policy & Reward Design

Drafting statutory handbooks, salary scales, and appraisal protocols.

03
System Implementation

Training managers on appraisal delivery, policy enforcement, and HR software.

04
Ongoing Advisory

Providing responsive counsel on employee relations, talent challenges, and compliance.
