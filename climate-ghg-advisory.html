Carbon Accounting

Verifiable Greenhouse Gas Accounting & Science-Based Footprints

Accurate carbon accounting is the foundational prerequisite for any credible decarbonization strategy. We assist enterprises in calculating their operational and value chain greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions strictly in accordance with the international GHG Protocol Corporate Standard, providing verifiable baselines for stakeholder disclosure.

Key Capabilities

What We Deliver

✓

Scope 1 Direct Emissions Accounting

Calculating emissions from company-owned facilities, stationary fuel combustion, corporate vehicle fleets, and refrigerants.

✓

Scope 2 Purchased Energy Emissions

Measuring location-based and market-based indirect emissions from purchased electricity, steam, heating, and cooling.

✓

Scope 3 Value Chain Footprint

Assessing indirect upstream and downstream emissions across purchased goods, freight transportation, and business travel.

✓

Science-Based Target Modeling (SBTi)

Modeling emissions reduction trajectories aligned with Paris Agreement climate science benchmarks.

Execution Lifecycle

Our Delivery Methodology

A disciplined, iterative delivery lifecycle engineered for clarity, speed, and accountability.

01

Boundary Definition

Determining operational and organizational control boundaries across all subsidiaries and facilities.

02

Activity Data Collection

Gathering utility utility bills, fuel consumption logs, travel mileage, and supply chain procurement volumes.

03

Emissions Calculation

Applying verified IPCC and regional emissions factors to compute total MT CO2e across Scopes 1, 2, and 3.

04

Footprint Inventory Report

Delivering an audit-defensible GHG Inventory Report complete with reduction recommendations.
